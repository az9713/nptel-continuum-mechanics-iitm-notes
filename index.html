Learning route and prerequisites

This edition assumes undergraduate linear algebra, multivariable calculus and Newtonian mechanics. It develops continuum-specific definitions and conventions in place. Read chapters 1–4 for the mathematical language, 5–6 for stress, 7–9 for kinematics, 10 for transport and balance, 11–14 for material models, and 15–16 for a complete application and synthesis. Chapters 17–20 are targeted companions to the four live discussions.

The same physical objects recur throughout: a material motion, its deformation gradient, an oriented surface and its traction, a material region and its balance laws. The notes distinguish a tensor from its component table, a physical rotation from a coordinate change, and a constitutive model from a conservation law. These distinctions become numerical checks in the examples and interactive laboratory.

Conventions used across the book

The stress convention is force-component first: tᵢ=σᵢⱼnⱼ. Velocity and displacement gradients also put the field component first and differentiation direction second. Some lecture segments and external references use the transpose convention; the chapters translate it explicitly. Tensile normal stress is positive and ordinary compressive pressure contributes −pI. Material labels are X, current positions x, F=∂x/∂X and J=detF. Energy normalization and stress configuration are stated where used.

Sources and original teaching material

Every playlist item is retained in the recording inventory, including the trailer, two lesson 6 uploads and four discussion sessions. Chapters follow the numbered prerequisites rather than reproduce the playlist’s misplaced lecture 11 and swapped lectures 53/54. The live discussions are numbered by their spoken introductions; their playlist positions are retained.

Locally generated English transcripts provide topic order and navigation anchors. They can misrecognize terminology and cannot reliably transcribe board equations. Original definitions, derivations, computational constructions and worked exercises make the notes independently readable. Inspected external sources are cited beside the claims they support. Full audiovisual transcription and human accessibility certification are not claimed.
